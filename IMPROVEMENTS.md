- **Primary Gradient**: Blue (#3b82f6) → Purple (#a855f7) → Pink (#ec4899)
- **Background**: Zinc-950 (#09090b)
- **Accent Colors**: Blue-500 to Purple-600 for highlights
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond (display), DM Sans (body)
- **Spacing**: Consistent padding and gap values
- **Borders**: Zinc-800 with gradient hover states
